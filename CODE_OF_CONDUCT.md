# Code of Conduct

CX contributors and community members are expected to keep participation
technical, respectful, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of experience, identity, background, or
  viewpoint.
- Critique code, evidence, claims, and design decisions rather than people.
- Reproduce command behavior before asserting that another contributor is
  wrong.
- Protect private data. Do not ask someone to publish credentials, databases,
  command histories, or failure artifacts.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussion focused on the reported scope.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately misleading benchmarks, fabricated output, or hidden telemetry.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after a maintainer has set scope.
- Using community channels to coordinate abuse or exploitation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/contextlimit). Maintainers may
edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban
accounts when needed to protect the project and its community.

Good-faith disagreement is welcome. Abuse is not.
